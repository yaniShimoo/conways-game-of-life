COMPLEXITY, FROM SIMPLICITY

Four rules.
A world of possibility.

Created by John Conway in 1970, this zero-player game evolves one generation at a time. Each cell has eight neighbors. You set the beginning.

B3 / S23 · Conway's original rules
01

Underpopulation

A living cell with fewer than 2 living neighbors dies.

02

Survival

A living cell with 2 or 3 living neighbors stays alive.

03

Overpopulation

A living cell with more than 3 living neighbors dies.

Reproduction
